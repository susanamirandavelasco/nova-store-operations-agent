# Business Case

## Client context

**NovaRetail** is a fictional Mexican electronics retailer created for this portfolio project.

For the simulated engagement, NovaRetail has:

- 35 stores
- approximately 450 frontline employees
- operational documentation covering returns, warranties, promotions, payment methods, and store procedures

These figures are hypothetical and are used only to model a realistic enterprise AI project.

## Problem statement

Frontline employees frequently need help while serving customers. Operational knowledge exists, but is distributed across manuals and documents.

Employees therefore escalate routine questions to supervisors, creating interruptions and slowing customer service.

For the simulated business case we assume:

- 300 active frontline employees per day
- 3 supervisor questions per employee per shift
- approximately 4 minutes per interruption

This represents roughly 60 hours of supervisor time per day.

The numbers are assumptions, not measured production data.

## Business objective

Reduce operational questions requiring supervisor intervention by **30%**, without increasing incorrect operational decisions.

## Proposed solution

Nova is a Special Purpose AI Agent focused on store operations.

Rather than being a general-purpose assistant, Nova has a constrained responsibility: help employees resolve operational questions and, eventually, perform selected low-risk actions under explicit business controls.

## Business value hypothesis

If Nova can reliably resolve common questions and assist with structured operational workflows, NovaRetail may:

- reduce supervisor interruptions
- decrease resolution time
- improve consistency in applying policies
- improve employee self-service
- reduce procedural errors

These hypotheses must be validated during a pilot rather than assumed from technical performance alone.
